The Rich History of Shisha in Cairo

Hookah, known locally as shisha, has deep roots in Egyptian culture dating back centuries. Originally a symbol of hospitality, it evolved from simple water pipes to elaborate social rituals. In 2026, Cairo's scene honors these traditions while embracing innovative trends like herbal infusions and low-nicotine options. Many lounges preserve classic setups with brass pipes and fruit-based tobaccos, offering a glimpse into Egypt's heritage.
