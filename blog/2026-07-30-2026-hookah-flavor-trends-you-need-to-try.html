Emerging Flavor Profiles

One standout trend is the rise of botanical and herbal infusions. Flavors like lavender-mint and rosemary-citrus are gaining popularity for their calming effects, appealing to those seeking a relaxing session. Tropical combinations such as dragon fruit with basil or mango-passionflower also dominate menus. These new mixes move beyond classic mint and double apple, offering unique sensory journeys.

Another growing category involves dessert-inspired options with a healthy spin. Think matcha-coconut or dark chocolate with sea salt, which pair well with lighter tobacco blends.
