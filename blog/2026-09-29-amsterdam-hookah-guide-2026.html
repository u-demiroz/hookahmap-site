Top Neighborhoods for Hookah in Amsterdam

The Red Light District features energetic lounges with international crowds, while De Pijp offers intimate settings perfect for relaxed sessions. Oud-West stands out for its creative flavor menus inspired by local ingredients like stroopwafel notes. HookahMap users frequently highlight these areas for their variety and accessibility via public transport.

  • Canal Ring: Scenic views paired with classic mint and apple mixes
  • Jordaan: Artistic interiors and craft tobacco selections
  • Nieuwmarkt: Late-night spots popular among young professionals
