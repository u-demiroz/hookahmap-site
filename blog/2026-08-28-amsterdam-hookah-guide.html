The Jordaan area stands out for its intimate settings and traditional shisha setups. Cafes here often feature fruit-infused options alongside classic mint and apple mixes. Meanwhile, the De Pijp neighborhood brings a trendy edge with experimental techniques like layered bowl preparations.
